Add user

Mobile is mandatory — it is the sign-in id on the phone when a device QR is scanned (OTP sign-in works for everyone). Email is optional, for OTP by email. The user sets their own password on first login. Operators bind Passport QRs from the scan page; an Operator with the approve tick also approves unlock requests from the portal or phone.

Person

Role & scope

Operator is one role for everyone who handles goods and scans: supplyman, collector, picker, dispatcher, delivery, warehouse, approver. The type is a label shown next to the name; it does not change permissions. Every Operator can sign in on the phone from a device QR and bind Passport QRs or make the delivery scan. Edit roles
