Demo script · 2 minutes

1Scan TRK-0418 (device QR) → Operator scan page. Show the Passport QR list and tap Bind 24 Passport QRs.
2Bound · leg starts. Tap Delivered · scan at the stop → stop 1 of 2 at Apollo Pharmacy: only 011234 and 011235 are handed over, the other three continue.
3Scan PP-AD-011234 (Passport QR) → what the patient / pharmacist sees: no login, English / हिन्दी, whole chain anonymised, verification token. Tap Open the PDF report.
4Scan PP-AD-011236 → same record for an item still on the way (last step "in progress", generation time shown).
5Scan TRK-0401 (HUB device QR) → the same scan page used for storage: Bind 120 to Cold room A · storage starts.
6Open the portal Dashboard on a laptop: the same device and items appear live — excursion on TRK-0418, journey by device, Passport QR stock.

Wireframe links only: the real product resolves a device QR to trackotic.com/d/<token> and a Passport QR to trackotic.com/p/<token>; the id is never in the QR.
